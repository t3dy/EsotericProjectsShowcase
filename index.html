Knowledge Portals — Digital Humanities Databases
🦅
AtalantaClaudiens
A site on Michael Maier's Atalanta Fugiens (1618), an alchemical emblem book with 50 engraved plates. Each emblem has its own page with the image, Maier's Latin epigram, and interpretation notes drawn from De Jong, Tilton, Craven, Wescott, and Pagel. Also includes an alchemical dictionary, a source map tracing Maier's classical and alchemical references, a reception timeline from 1618 to the present, and a full bibliography.

TheosophicalAlchemyDB
A knowledge portal on Rosicrucian and theosophical-alchemical traditions from the 16th through 18th century. Draws primarily on Yates, Vickers, and Szulakowska. Organized around 206 structured entities — 63 figures, 60 concepts, 83 texts, and 30+ emblems — with individual pages for each. Includes an interactive map, a card gallery, and essay pages on major themes.

MedievalMagicDB
A knowledge portal on medieval magic covering learned ritual practice, astral image magic, necromancy, angelic theurgy, grimoires, and manuscript transmission. Organized with explicit analyst-category distinctions — it tracks what practitioners claimed separately from what historians argue — and includes summaries of major figures, texts, and traditions, along with a dictionary of terms and a historiography section.

Renaissance Magic DB
A knowledge portal on Renaissance magic covering Dee, Ficino, Pico, Agrippa, Bruno, Fludd, Trithemius, Reuchlin, Kircher, and Böhme. Built from a corpus of 337 cataloged scholarly documents. Includes biographical pages for 29 historical figures, a dictionary of 139 terms (Latin, Greek, Hebrew, Arabic), a timeline of 58 events, and pages for 9 intellectual traditions including Hermeticism, Kabbalah, Neoplatonism, and Enochian practice.

WitchcraftStudiesDB
A knowledge portal on historical witchcraft studies drawing on Stuart Clark (Thinking with Demons), Ronald Hutton, and Carlo Ginzburg. Covers 320+ structured entities: 40+ documented trials, 40+ accused individuals, 30+ demonological concepts, 40+ scholars and texts, and 40+ locations. Seven pages organized for three audiences — scholars who want historiographical precision, students who want accessible context, and researchers who want full browsability.

CrowleyDB
A research database on Aleister Crowley covering his works, biography, associates, travels, and occult system. Includes a full corpus catalog, Tree of Life correspondences, grade and number attributions, a timeline and location browser, tarot readings, and an initiation game. Source attribution is explicit throughout — primary records, scholarly commentary, and practitioner interpretation are kept distinct.

Emblem Print Shop
An image parts library for alchemical visual elements extracted from emblem books by Maier, Cramer, Stolcius, and Khunrath. 7,000+ objects — dragons, lions, vessels, furnaces, suns, moons, trees, hermaphrodites, kings and queens — tagged, sourced, and browsable by corpus and motif. Built using GroundingDINO + SAM for extraction. Also includes an emblem catalog (136 emblems), a motif atlas (65 controlled-vocabulary entries), and a reference section covering ~30 major alchemical image sequences from ancient Egypt through 1788.

Goetia Diagram Processing
An image processing pipeline for historical Goetia ritual diagrams. Uses OpenCV for preprocessing, edge detection, contour extraction, feature analysis, and pattern matching. The goal is to extract structured data from scanned historical diagrams — identifying geometric forms, sigils, and repeated visual elements across the corpus.

Almagest — Digby Game
A construction kit for building historical RPG scenarios from scholarly sources. Converts primary-source notes into playable scenes through five layers: source analysis, entity ontology, reusable primitives (scenes, actors, encounters, resources), deterministic world compilation, and a React/Zustand runtime. Supports three play modes: systemic reputation-gated choices (Ultima-style), scripted dramatic scenes (FF-style), and resource crisis management (FTL-style). First dataset: Kenelm Digby, 1628. Electron desktop app.

Tree Tapper
A set of HTML game prototypes built around the Tree of Life. Covers text adventures, resource-tapping mechanics, shrinking/growth sequences, and branching progression through the Sephiroth. Uses Kabbalistic color scales, Hebrew attributions, and tarot correspondences as design reference. Iterated across a series of standalone HTML files.
